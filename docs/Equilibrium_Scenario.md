# Equilibrium: a story joining Omega Ruby and X

The owner's request (16 October): a "what if" story, not canon, for a mod that joins Hoenn (Omega Ruby) and Kalos (X).
Deep, original, coherent, with large themes and characters who change. Two constraints from the owner: **no new
characters**, only those of ORAS and X/Y (their models are the game's; Pomegrade cannot make new ones), and **no
Sinnoh**. The meteor of ORAS's Delta Episode is placed in the middle of the game, not after it.

What the technical side allows is in `tools/remake/ORAS_ENGINE.md` section 7 (X's assets beside Omega Ruby's). What
is canon and what is invented is marked in section 8.

## 1. The question and the themes

**The question**: what do we owe to what we have lost?

- **Grief.** AZ lost Floette 3,000 years ago; Zinnia lost Aster, her Whismur. The same wound, met in opposite ways.
- **Giving and taking.** Mega Evolution and AZ's weapon run on one energy. Mega Evolution is that energy shared freely
  between a trainer and a Pokémon who trust each other; the weapon tears it out of Pokémon by force.
- **No one comes back the same.** What is taken to bring the dead back returns an empty shell; what is given does not
  bring back the one who left, it gives birth to someone new.

## 2. The chain of causes

1. A meteor heads for the world; Rayquaza destroys it in the middle of the game.
2. Its shards fall on Hoenn and Kalos, full of raw energy.
3. Xerosic makes the corrupted orbs from those shards.
4. Rayquaza, worn out by the meteor, is too weak when the corrupted Primal forms wake.
5. It dies, and Zygarde decides its fate.

Each act is born from the one before; nothing happens by chance.

## 3. The legendaries: two pairs, two arbiters

| Pair | Opposition | Arbiter |
|---|---|---|
| Groudon / Kyogre | land / sea | Rayquaza |
| Xerneas / Yveltal | life / death | Zygarde |

The corrupted orbs cross the pairs:

| Orb | Pokémon | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Crimson Orb** (Yveltal's essence) | Groudon | The land dries out, then turns to stone: life is taken |
| **Azure Orb** (Xerneas's essence) | Kyogre | Life overflows and nothing can die: the towns drown in plants, the wounded stay suspended |

Neither arbiter can undo a crossed pair alone, which is why Rayquaza falls and Zygarde has to act.

Rayquaza keeps the balance by **separation**: land and sea held apart. Zygarde keeps it by the **cycle**: life and death
bound, each feeding the other. The crossed orbs mix the pairs, and separating no longer works: the story moves the world
from the wall to the cycle. Rayquaza stands between the two men like a wall; Zygarde holds both like a cycle.

## 3.1 The visions

Each one refuses a part of the cycle. The characters' ages come from the story's new chronology (Archie older, Maxie
younger than in ORAS; how it is placed is still to settle) and from the themes: the old man fears his end, the young
man has not yet learnt that things are lost.

| Character | Vision | Refuses |
|---|---|---|
| **Archie** (old), Kyogre and the Azure Orb (Xerneas) | A life that never ends: the sea where nothing dies | Death |
| **Maxie** (young), Groudon and the Crimson Orb (Yveltal) | A perfect world fixed in stone, where nothing decays | Change, wear |
| **Lysandre** | Owning life and death, for himself, in the name of all; he is dying | Losing anything; he takes the fear of one and the dream of the other |
| **The hero** | Consent: the strength that is given, never taken (the Key Stone given in act II, Rayquaza let go in act V); Zygarde's human counterpart | Nothing; their trial is path A, becoming Lysandre |
| **Zinnia** | Faithfulness to what was lost: Aster, her people's prophecy | Letting go; AZ shows her where refusing leads |
| **Wally** | The hero's mirror, the same journey with another temptation: forcing strength, Lysandre's taking at a small scale | Being weak again |

Lysandre's mirror is AZ, who did the same 3,000 years ago and still pays for it.

## 4. The acts

### Act I, Hoenn: the warning
- Brendan or May begins their journey; Wally, a former sickly child afraid of becoming weak again, is the rival.
- The Draconids, through Zinnia, the Lorekeeper, announce a meteor within months.
- Steven works out that Mega Evolution comes from the same energy as the meteor.
- Maxie and Archie, haunted by the Primal disaster they caused, are visited by a man from Kalos thought dead: Lysandre.
  He needs them themselves: the orbs answer only those who once woke a Primal form. He speaks to what each one fears:
  to old Archie, a life that never ends; to young Maxie, a world that no longer wears away (section 3.1).
- End: Zinnia steals Key Stones, Steven's among them, to summon Rayquaza, as her people's prophecy demands.

### Act II, the sky: what we give
- The player climbs the Sky Pillar with Zinnia. Rayquaza is too weak to Mega Evolve; the player gives their own Key
  Stone and their Mega Evolution: the game's first gift.
- Mega Rayquaza destroys the meteor. In space, Deoxys, born from the meteor's energy, fights it to a draw and falls with
  the debris.
- The meteor has not vanished: it shattered, and its shards rain on Hoenn and Kalos. Rayquaza returns to its pillar,
  drained.
- The prophecy is fulfilled, and Zinnia's life has lost its purpose. She disappears, still grieving Aster.

### Act III, Kalos: what we take
- The trail of the shards leads to Kalos: Lumiose, Sycamore, Korrina, Diantha. The player goes because Steven asks them
  to: a shard was sold to a Lumiose collector, and Steven, who knows Lysandre, will not say why he fears it.
- AZ tells his story: the war, Floette, the weapon, the stolen lives. Korrina learns that her family's Mega Stones were
  born from the Pokémon sacrificed on Route 10.
- Lysandre survived the weapon's collapse, kept alive by its energy as AZ was. He no longer wants to destroy the world;
  he wants to end death, because he is dying slowly and only the weapon's energy keeps him alive.
- Xerosic gathers the shards and drains Yveltal's and Xerneas's essences, asleep as a cocoon and a tree, to make the
  Crimson and Azure Orbs.
- Malva, of the Elite Four, is The Dawn's mole inside the League. Her clue: she knows of the player's Kalos visit before
  anyone was told.
- Steven confesses that he gave Lysandre his research on Mega Evolution years ago.
- Zinnia reappears at Lysandre's side: he has promised her Aster back.

### Act IV, Hoenn: the fall
- Maxie uses the Crimson Orb on Groudon, sure he is repairing his mistake. The land dries and turns to stone.
- Lysandre offers the Azure Orb as the cure; Archie, afraid of his own end, uses it on Kyogre. Life overflows and
  nothing can die.
- Rayquaza comes down from the sky to part them, as the legend says, still drained by the meteor. Groudon takes its
  life, Kyogre keeps it from dying: it falls, neither dead nor alive.
- Wally wants to force things; his Gallade refuses to Mega Evolve. His crisis, then his growth.
- Zinnia finds Aster again in Archie's flood, where nothing dies: its body comes back, not Aster. The Whismur looks at
  her and does not know her.

### Act V: equilibrium
- Zygarde wakes. With Rayquaza fallen, land and sea have no arbiter, life and death are tangled; it means to remove the
  cause, the humans.
- Zinnia meets AZ, who did the same 3,000 years ago and tells her its price: the emotional heart of the game.
- The choice before Rayquaza's body:
  - **Path A** (bad ending): force it back with the orbs' energy. Rayquaza rises empty and obedient; Lysandre was right,
    and the player has become him.
  - **Path B** (true ending): let Rayquaza die for real; Zygarde gives its core; Rayquaza is born again, different,
    green and black with Zygarde's patterns (**Rayquaza of Equilibrium**); Zygarde breaks into cells scattered over both
    regions.
- Final battle: Rayquaza of Equilibrium against the corrupted Primal forms. It draws out Xerneas's and Yveltal's
  essences and gives them back; Groudon and Kyogre go back to sleep.
- Lysandre lives only by the weapon's energy; the player can cut it, and he dies at peace. Xerosic is arrested, Malva
  unmasked.
- Archie accepts that he will die; Maxie accepts that what he builds will wear away. Both orbs go dark.

### Epilogue
- AZ gives up his long life; Floette comes back to him for his last hours.
- Zinnia buries Aster a second time, by choice.
- Korrina and Steven raise a memorial on Route 10 to the sacrificed Pokémon.
- Wally wins the League without Mega Evolution.
- After the story: finding Zygarde's cells in both regions to make it whole again; meeting Deoxys, unseen since its fall.

## 6. What it asks of the game

- **Characters**: every one above must be found among the field models (`a/0/2/1` in both games); X's need a BCH 0x07
  reader and conversion first (`tools/remake/ORAS_ENGINE.md` section 7).
- **Rayquaza on the ground, the corrupted Primal forms, Rayquaza of Equilibrium**: repaints of Omega Ruby's Rayquaza,
  Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre models (BCH 0x21, the version the tools read and write); no new shapes. A lying pose
  needs an existing animation (fainting is a candidate, not checked).
- **Zygarde**: X/Y and Omega Ruby have only its 50% Forme; it stays in that form.
- **The new orbs, forms and their battle data**: editing the game's item and Pokémon tables, which `tools/remake` cannot
  do yet.
- **Kalos's places** (Lumiose, Route 10, Geosenge): X's map pieces brought into Omega Ruby (section 7's order of work).
- **The choice between paths A and B, every scene**: scripts of the mod's own, not built yet (`ORAS_ENGINE.md` section 0,
  the sandbox).

## 8. Canon and invention

From the games, checked on Bulbapedia (16 October):
- AZ's war 3,000 years ago, Floette made immortal, the weapon powered by Xerneas's and/or Yveltal's energy (the sources
  differ), the lives taken, the graves on Route 10, Mega Stones made by the blast.
- Lysandre reactivating the weapon from Lysandre Labs; Xerosic firing it by remote control.
- The Delta Episode's meteor, the Draconids, Zinnia as Lorekeeper, Rayquaza too weak to Mega Evolve without the player.
- The Red and Blue Orbs triggering Primal Reversion, the Cave of Origin's crystals.
- From the anime (Mega Evolution Specials): Lysandre and Steven researching Mega Evolution together.

Recalled, not checked against a source: Zinnia's Whismur named Aster; Yveltal's Pokédex entry about absorbing life;
Yveltal turning living things to stone in the film *Diancie and the Cocoon of Destruction*.

Invented for this story: Lysandre surviving and wanting to end death, the meteor's shards as the orbs' material, Deoxys
born from the meteor's energy, the Crimson and Azure Orbs, Rayquaza's death, Zygarde's core and Rayquaza of
Equilibrium, The Dawn, Malva as its mole, Steven's share of the blame, Archie older and Maxie younger (their recoloured
models, 16 October), the orbs answering only those who woke a Primal form, Archie using the Azure Orb, the shard sold in
Lumiose.
